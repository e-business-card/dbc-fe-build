A Digital business Card is a smart, shareable card that lets you connect, share contact details, or link to your business or profile instantly using a simple QR code.

It is a digital business card you create and share from a web browser, so there is no app to download, for you or for the people you share with. Share it as a QR code or a link and anyone can save your contact with one tap. It is also known as a virtual business card or an electronic business card.

Digital QR Card has no monthly subscription. The Free plan gives you 2 digital cards with unlimited sharing. Pro is a one-time payment of $45 for lifetime access.
