Global Privacy Policy for DigitalQRCard

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

Governing Language

This Privacy Policy is published in multiple languages for your convenience. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between the English version and any translated version, the English version shall prevail and be the binding document.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked with you ("Personal Data").

  • Identifiers: Name, email, IP address, and unique device identifiers. IP addresses are truncated/anonymized so we retain only country or region-level information (not precise location) to comply with privacy regulations.
  • Commercial Information: Transaction history and booking details.
  • Internet Activity: Interaction data within the app and usage statistics.
  • Site assistant: your questions are answered by an AI model from a third-party provider, which receives each question and, when you are signed in, a few facts about your account such as your plan (never your email or payment details). We also keep the questions you type, with email addresses, phone numbers and card numbers removed, for 90 days to improve the answers and, when you are signed in, linked to your account so we can help you. A question about a payment, or about something you paid for not working, may be passed to our team so a person can follow up.
  • Sensitive Data: Account login credentials (encrypted).

2. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your data under the following legal bases:

  • Contractual Necessity: To provide the DigitalQRCard services you signed up for.
  • Legitimate Interests: To improve our services, maintain security, and prevent fraud.
  • Consent: Where you have explicitly opted-in (e.g., to receive marketing emails or for non-essential cookies).
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

3. Your Global Privacy Rights

Regardless of your location, you have rights regarding your data. Residents of California (CCPA/CPRA), Europe (GDPR), Canada (PIPEDA), and Brazil (LGPD) have specific protections:

RightDescription
Access & PortabilityRequest a copy of your data in a machine-readable format.
CorrectionRequest that we fix inaccurate or incomplete information.
Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten")Request that we erase your personal data, subject to legal limits.
Opt-Out of Selling/SharingDirect us not to share your data for targeted advertising.
Withdraw ConsentRevoke your consent for data processing at any time.

4. International Data Transfers

DigitalQRCard is headquartered in the United States. If you are accessing our services from outside the U.S., your data will be transferred to our servers in the U.S.

Safeguards: For users in the EEA and UK, we utilize Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as approved by the European Commission to ensure your data receives the same level of protection it would within your home country.

5. Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell your personal data for money. However, we may "share" identifiers with analytics and advertising partners to improve your experience.

Global Privacy Control (GPC): Our systems are designed to recognize and honor GPC signals from your browser as a valid request to opt-out of sharing.

Manual Opt-Out: You may also opt-out via the "Privacy" menu in your app settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in Section 2. When data is no longer required, we either delete it or permanently anonymize it so it cannot be linked back to you.

7. Contact Us

To exercise any of your rights or ask a question about this policy:

Email: support@digitalqrcard.com
