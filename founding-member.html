Questions before you lock it in

How is this different from Popl, HiHello, Linq, or Blinq?

Those are tap-to-share digital cards, and ours works the same way. What we add is a voice layer: visitors tap Talk to me and have a real-time voice conversation with an AI agent trained on your profile, in your cloned voice or a ready-made one. The Voice Agent is in private beta. Built on ElevenLabs Conversational AI.

Do the people scanning need to install an app?

No. They tap or scan with the camera on any phone — iPhone or Android — and your full profile opens instantly. They can save your contact, or tap Talk to me, in one motion.
