What we’re building

Digital QR Card started as a customizable digital business card and is becoming an AI operating system for solo and small-team founders — a layer of context, data, intelligence, and automation wrapped around a real business so it can run leaner than any competitor.

The daily brief that arrives in the founder’s inbox every morning is the first published layer. Voice agents, product demos, content pipelines, and vertical launches (real estate first) are next. The bet is that AI-native teams of one to five can now do what agencies of forty used to.

If that’s the kind of company you want to build inside of, we should talk.

How we work

  • Weekly ships. Every Friday something goes live — a landing page, a feature, a campaign, a video. If nothing shipped, we broke the week.
  • Voice-first drafting, written follow-through. Talk your first draft, edit it into a doc, commit the doc. Meetings only when a doc can’t do the job.
  • Numbers are open. Revenue, churn, error counts, brief digest — everyone on the team sees the same dashboard the founder sees.
  • Contract-first hiring. We start every relationship as a paid contract engagement (usually 4–12 weeks). If we both want more, we move to a longer commitment with equity in the mix.
  • Async, remote, worldwide. No required overlap hours. Pick the hours that make your life better and deliver against the ship schedule.
