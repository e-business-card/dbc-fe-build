A digital business card built around how real estate agents actually work: sign riders, open houses, listing flyers, and every other place you hand out your info. One scan, you're saved, you're tracked, you can follow up Monday.

Real estate is the profession where a business card has to do five jobs at once: sign rider, open-house signup, listing flyer, social bio, and post-close referral asset. A paper card does one of those well. Every brokerage change means another $50 reprint and a drawer full of cards with last year's phone number. I built Digital QR Card because the agents I talked to during my own home search kept handing me business cards that were already wrong — old brokerage, old number, old title. The premise here is simple: your card should be a URL you control, not a piece of paper you reprint. Sign rider QR scans land on your full profile with one-tap save to contacts, lead capture before the visitor leaves, and a same-day notification when someone scans from a property. Open house follow-up moves off the clipboard. License-number and brokerage fields exist as first-class fields so your card meets state advertising rules without bolt-on text.

How this works for you

Sign rider QR code

Print your QR on every sign rider. Drivers who scan land on your card with your contact, your listings, and a one-tap save. The lead-capture form catches their email before they leave.

Open house follow-up

Tent card on the sign-in table replaces the clipboard. Visitors scan, enter their email, you get their contact same-day with the property they were at. Export to your CRM Monday morning.

Change brokerage, update in 10 seconds

New brokerage, new title, new phone. Update once on the dashboard, every card you've ever handed out shows the new info. No reprints, no wasted boxes of stale cards.

Real estate is the highest-impact use case in this category. Fifty cards at every open house, brokerage changes every 2-3 years, every contact saved means a future referral. Paper can't keep up.

Common questions

Does the card show my license number and brokerage info?

Yes. Required-by-most-states fields (agent license number, brokerage name, brokerage license) all have dedicated slots. You add them once, they're on every share.

What happens to my QR codes when I change brokerages?

Your card URL stays the same. Update your brokerage and title in the dashboard, and every existing sign rider, flyer, and business card already handed out now shows the new info. Zero reprints.

Can I put a different QR on each listing?

Not yet. For now, your main card QR covers all listings, and visitors can see your full inventory once they scan. Per-listing tracking is on the roadmap.

How do leads get into my CRM?

Lead capture stores them in your dashboard with a one-click CSV export. Import into your CRM (Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, Sierra Interactive, BoomTown) Monday morning after the open house.

How much does the QR sign rider cost?

We don't sell physical products. Send the QR file from your dashboard to Lowen, ABCO, or FastSigns. A standard QR rider runs $15-40 depending on the printer.

Will my older clients actually scan a QR code?

Honest answer: most will, some won't. Every iPhone since iOS 11 (2017) scans QRs from the native camera. Same on Android since 2019. For the holdouts, text or email them the link instead. Once your card is open, they can add it to their home screen and it sits with their apps, one tap away after that.
