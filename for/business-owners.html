Small business owners wear too many hats for a single business card to be honest. You're the founder when you talk to investors, the salesperson when you talk to prospects, the head of support when an existing customer has a problem, and the operations lead when a vendor calls. Each of those audiences needs different framing, different links, different proof points. A paper card forces you to pick one identity and stamp it on a rectangle. A digital card lets you carry several cards under the same dashboard and pull up the right one for the conversation in front of you. Team-side: when you hire your first sales rep, your first support person, your first contractor, the question of how they show up to the world matters. Crew packs let you buy 3, 5, or 10 lifetime seats so each team member gets their own card with their own contacts and analytics, optionally sharing a brand color palette across the team. Owner sees aggregate scan counts to know what's working — no per-person PII surveillance, no monthly bill that scales linearly with headcount.

Common questions

How does the Crew pack pricing work?

One-time purchase: $99 for 3 seats, $149 for 5, $249 for 10. Owner is included in the seat count. Each member gets full Pro features (lead capture, analytics, CSV export) for the lifetime of the seat.

Can my team members keep their cards if they leave my company?

Their card revokes from the team seat when you remove them. They can continue on a free or paid individual account, but the team seat returns to the pool for someone else.

Do team members see each other's contacts?

No. Each member's leads and analytics are their own. The owner sees aggregate counts but no PII.

Can I rebrand the entire team's cards with my logo?

Yes. Team-level branding lets you set a logo and two brand colors that apply across every team card. Optional per-member opt-out.

What if I need more than 10 seats?

Email us at support@digitalqrcard.com with your team size. We're working on a larger tier for 25+ seats with full admin tools, SSO, and custom domain.
