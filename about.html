Meet the founder

I'm Ilyes Tounsi, based in Carlsbad, California. I built Digital QR Card because every time I changed roles, brokerages, or phone numbers, I ended up with a drawer of dead paper cards. The contacts on the cards I'd already handed out couldn't reach the current me. The math kept feeling absurd: a single typo or new title meant another $50 order, and the old stock went straight to the recycling bin.

Digital QR Card is the version I wanted to exist. One scan, full profile, updates in seconds, no app required. Paid once at $45 lifetime — no monthly bill, no expiring trial, no upsell pressure. Built specifically for real-estate agents, sales reps, and small-business operators who hand out cards by the hundred and refuse to keep paying for the same problem.

I write the code, answer the support emails, and ship the updates myself. Solo operator. Building in public. If something on the site doesn't work, email support@digitalqrcard.com — it reaches me directly, usually within a few hours during the US workday.

What this product doesn't do

I want to be honest about what you're not buying. Digital QR Card is not a CRM, not a marketing-automation suite, and not a directory. It doesn't auto-post to your social channels or run ad campaigns. It does one job — replace the paper business card with a scannable digital identity — and it does that one job well. If you need a full pipeline tool, this pairs with whatever CRM you already use; it doesn't replace it.

— Ilyes, Carlsbad, CA
